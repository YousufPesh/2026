What the audit brings into view

Look beyond how the page appears

  • Document structure. Are headings, paragraphs, lists, and tables identified by their roles?
  • Reading order. Does the content follow a meaningful sequence?
  • Content alternatives. Can meaningful images, charts, and formulas be understood beyond their visual presentation?
Why this matters for this document

A page can look organized while its underlying structure is missing.

Choose the approach for the document

01

Preserve layout

  • Keep the familiar page design when the visual layout already works.
  • Add or correct tags, heading hierarchy, lists, tables, and reading order.
  • Use for documents whose main need is a better underlying structure.
02

Full reconstruction

  • Rebuild the presentation when the content needs more than tagging.
  • Turn scans or handwritten material into structured text and formulas.
  • Review rebuilt content closely against the original, especially notation and meaning.
