01 EVERYONE, OR NOBODY

AI shouldn't be something you can afford.

Individual subscriptions sort a campus by who can pay $20 a month. One licence removes the question entirely.

02 EVERY MODEL

Not one vendor. All of them.

One licence. No separate contract per vendor.

Frontier and open models side by side, so a department is never stuck with whichever one the institution happened to sign first.

03 SWITCH MID-THREAD

Change model. Keep the conversation.

One thread. The context carries across every model.

Ask one model to draft, another to check it, a cheaper one to summarise. Nobody copies anything between tabs.

04 BRING PEOPLE IN

A conversation, not a private tab.

05 MESSAGE MODE

Turn the AI off. It still remembers.

Talk among yourselves, decide something, then bring the AI back. It picks up with everything that was said while it was quiet.
