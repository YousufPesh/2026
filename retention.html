01 NOTICE WHAT CHANGES

Recognize early signs that a student may need support.

Changes in coursework or participation can be easy to miss when they appear in different systems. Bring those signals together to help your team decide when to reach out.

Where would you focus?

A signal is a reason to check in. It does not explain the student’s whole situation.
